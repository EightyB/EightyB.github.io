Upgrading Software Help

If your current software setup is inadequate, EightyB can help you take it where you want it to be.

I will analyse your existing processes and:

1. Add functionality while combinining and preserving user interaction.

2. Develop a bespoke application around the way your business actually works.

Single Pc systems can be converted to multi user Server-Client systems, where a central server database holds the status information, read and modified from the clients (your Users' Pc's). A Warehouse Crane Controller is shown below.


For example, in Meat Processing, this could include production, stock, weighing, labelling, recipes, invoicing and traceability, bringing these processes together into a single application available to all your users.
