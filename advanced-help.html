Advanced Software Help

If your current software setup is inadequate, EightyB can help you take it where you want it to be.

I can analyse your existing processes and, where appropriate, develop a bespoke application around the way your business actually works.

Single Pc systems can be converted to multi user Server-Client systems, where a central server database holds the status information, read and modified from the clients (your Users' Pc's)

For example, in Meat Processing, this could include production, stock, weighing, labelling, recipes, invoicing and traceability, bringing these processes together into a single application available to all your users.
