About EightyB

EightyB Logistics - Alan Smith is a Self Trading company based in Poland for myself, Alan Smith. I am a Scot who travels between Warsaw and Perth, and have previously worked in larger companies in Software Engineering. I have had enough of large-scale computing, where the pace of change is small, and am now looking to smaller companies who need a leg-up electronically, whether in a basic move to Pc, or more specialist bespoke software.

EightyB Associates

I have a loose network of old friends and colleagues who are, in the main, like myself, long-in-the-tooth software engineers, looking to help your company. If their help is required, then you pay them (their company) for their efforts.

Our focus

We focus on SME's across the UK and Europe. We keep costs as low as possible - cut to the bone, as they say in Meat Processing. Most work can be online, and done remotely, with only occasional visits where required. Chatting online saves time and money.
