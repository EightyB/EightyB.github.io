About EightyB

EightyB is a Self Trading company based in Poland for myself, Alan Smith. I am a Scot who travels between Warsaw and Perth, and have previously worked in larger companies in Software Engineering. I have had enough of large-scale computing, where the pace of change is small, and am now looking to smaller companies who need a leg-up electronically, whether in a basic move to Pc, or more advanced bespoke software.

I focus on SME's across the UK and Europe. I am getting long in the tooth, born in 1965, so like to make use of my long-time colleagues should work build up.

My approach is simple: understand how a business works, identify where software can help, and build practical solutions that deliver simple-to-use applications.
