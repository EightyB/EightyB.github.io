Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you teach IB Computer Science HL and SL?

Yes. Tuition is available for both Higher Level and Standard Level students. The content and pace are adapted to the student's course and needs.

Do you teach IB1 and IB2?

Yes. IB1 students can use lessons to build strong foundations and stay on top of the course, while IB2 students can focus on consolidation, assessment and exam preparation.

Can you help with the Internal Assessment?

Yes. Support can include discussing ideas, understanding requirements, reviewing work and providing feedback. The student's submitted work must remain their own.

Do I need to be in Warsaw?

No. Online lessons are available, so students can work with EightyB from anywhere.

Is this suitable for a student who is struggling?

Yes. The first step is to identify where the difficulty lies. Lessons can then concentrate on missing foundations, specific topics, programming problems or exam technique.

Can lessons be intensive before exams?

Yes. Intensive revision can be arranged around particular topics, past-paper practice and the student's remaining areas of difficulty.
