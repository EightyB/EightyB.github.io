The focus

More than learning to code.

IB Computer Science requires students to understand concepts, reason about problems, write and interpret programs, and explain their thinking clearly.

Tuition is therefore focused on understanding the Computer Science behind the question, not simply producing a working piece of code.

Sessions are tailored to the student's current level, the areas they find difficult, and the demands of their IB course.

Two years · different needs

Support throughout the Diploma Programme.

The emphasis changes as students progress through the course.

IB1

Build understanding

Establish a strong foundation in programming, algorithms and Computer Science concepts. Work through difficult topics before gaps become problems later in the course.

IB2

Consolidate & prepare

Strengthen understanding, practise applying concepts to unfamiliar problems, and prepare systematically for IB assessment and exams.

Experience

Learn from someone who has taught Computer Science.

I have experience developing and teaching an International Baccalaureate Computer Science course.

My approach combines practical programming experience with the need to understand and communicate Computer Science concepts clearly.

The aim is not simply to make a program work. Students should understand why it works, be able to explain it, and apply the same thinking to a different problem.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you teach both HL and SL?

Yes. Tuition is available for both IB Computer Science Higher Level and Standard Level students.

Do you work with both IB1 and IB2 students?

Yes. The focus of sessions is adapted to the student's stage of the Diploma Programme.

Can you help with the student's coursework?

Guidance, explanation and feedback can be provided to help students understand requirements and improve their own work. Student work remains the student's own.

Can sessions be used for exam preparation?

Yes. Sessions can focus on examination-style questions, identifying gaps in understanding and practising the application of Computer Science concepts.

Where do lessons take place?

Tuition is available online and, where practical, in Warsaw.
