School Tutoring

EightyB Tutoring provides tutoring in Computer Science for secondary school pupils.
EightyB Tutoring is English-based, available to any students of Computer Science. Online tutoring is available to all; In-person classes are available in Warsaw, Poland.

While the school syllabus may vary in secondary schools (Polish: szkoła średnia), tutoring is available to all secondary pupils who wish to improve their grades.

Tutoring will focus on any specific aspects the students wish to strengthen.

For IB or state schooling, Core or Advanced diploma, We can explore how computers work, from binary code to high level languages. From basic concepts like pseudcode to more advanced concepts like binary trees, I can help.

For IB, the syllabus language is Java. We are not restricted. Perhaps you wish to use another language, such as C#.

Night or online classes will be set up according to schedule availability.
