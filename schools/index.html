Support Teaching and Tuition

Tuition.

Support Teaching.

IB Computer Science requires students to understand concepts, reason about problems, write and interpret programs, and explain their thinking clearly.

Tuition: I focus on understanding the Computer Science behind the question, not simply producing a working piece of code. Again frequent homework, but not lengthy. Sessions are tailored to the student's current level, the areas they find difficult, and the demands of their IB course.

Support Teaching: I can step in to any class and teach the lesson according to syllabus. I will also provide homework (not always, students will be glad to know), mark and return, with the aim of best helping the students understand the underlying principles behind the lesson topic.

Two years · different needs

Guidance throughout the Diploma Programme.

The emphasis changes as students progress through the course.

IB1

Build understanding

Establish a strong foundation in programming, algorithms and Computer Science concepts. Work through difficult topics before gaps become problems later in the course.

IB2

Consolidate & prepare

Strengthen understanding, practise applying concepts to unfamiliar problems, and prepare systematically for IB assessment and exams.

Tuition

IB Computer Science, HL and SL Syllabus Tutoring.

Focusing on the student's needs, tuition can be concentrated on one difficult topic and alternayive ways to gain understanding. Tuition can also be spread across the course.

01

Programming

Understand program structure, logic, debugging and problem solving.

02

Computer systems

Work through theoretical concepts and understand how they relate to computing systems.

03

Algorithms

Develop algorithmic thinking and learn to break down unfamiliar problems step-wise into reliable solutions.

04

Exam preparation

Practise applying knowledge to examination-style questions and identify areas requiring further work.

05

Coursework guidance

Academic guidance and feedback to help students understand requirements and improve their own work.

06

Student Focused

No rushing through topics. Student understanding is paramount. Simpler ways that suit the student are used in getting Syllabus concepts across.

For IB schools & teachers

Class Support Teaching.

If you are an IB Computer Science teacher or coordinator and would like support backup, I provide class support teaching for IB1 and IB2 students at HL and SL.

If you are a group of students who would benefit from additional teaching support, an additional support/tuition class can also be arranged.

The aim is to support the student's learning alongside your school's existing teaching. Personal in-class teaching is available in Warsaw, online elsewhere.

Experience

IB Computer Science Teaching Experience.

I set up and then taught the IB Diploma Computer Science course at IB Raszynska School for five years, teaching both HL and SL Computer Science.

In thirty-odd years of software engineering, I have worked across a vast sphere of industries, which provides some substance to syllabus teaching.

My approach focuses on getting across Computer Science concepts clearly and simply. I combine this with how these principles apply in assorted industries, from high quality Safety Critical to non-certified software.

The aim is to provide an understanding and appreciation of the IB syllabus in relation to how computers work.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you teach both HL and SL?

Yes. Tuition is available for both IB Computer Science Higher Level and Standard Level students.

Do you work with both IB1 and IB2 students?

Yes. The focus of sessions is adapted to the student's stage of the Diploma Programme.

Can you help with the student's coursework?

Guidance, explanation and feedback can be provided to help students understand requirements and improve their own work. Student work remains the student's own.

Can sessions be used for exam preparation?

Yes. Sessions can focus on examination-style questions, identifying gaps in understanding and practising the application of Computer Science concepts.

Where do lessons take place?

Tuition is available online and, where practical, in Warsaw.
