Basic Software Help

You may be starting up and unsure of what can be done on your PC, or recognise a need to move from a manual paper and pen system to using your pc.
If you are buying a Pc for businss, best go with Microsoft windows Pc's. Also get the Microsoft Office package.

Email and Letters

Letters are always better done on a Pc, especially as email replaces posted. You can set up an email address (Google gmail is always good) and use the hotmail directly to write your letters, though Word is the best tool here. You can set up a filing system and check your letters on the pc.
A simple lesson with some templates will get you going.

Invoices and Receipts

Invoices and Recipts are likely a part of your everyday. These can be done simply in Microsoft Excel.
A basic lesson with some templates will get you going.

Online

You may want to communicate online, and be able to view each other's Pc screen. This can be done simply with Microsoft Teams. Most businesses and institutions like schools use Teams. I use Teams. It was invaluable during Covid Lockdown. If you have kids, you'll know.
